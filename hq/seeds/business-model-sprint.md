# Cahier des charges — Business Model Sprint

## The question
What does this company cost to run, how does it make money, and when does it break even?

## Context (decided by the founder)
- The company is registered in **Tunisia** and is **100% virtual**: no office rent; the founder works from a home PC.
- It sells IT services to enterprises: software, cloud, cybersecurity, network, automation, AI integration, digital content and digital marketing. The work is delivered by AI agents, with the founder approving everything that leaves the company.
- Today it runs on **Claude Pro** (one agent at a time).
- All prices in **TND**, with **EUR and USD** alongside (many tools bill in USD; clients may be abroad).

## Deliverables per team
| Team | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| Finance & Projects (lead) | Cost model: Claude subscription/API tiers, hosting for HQ when it moves to the cloud, domain and email, CRM, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, design and security tools, hardware (workstation, backup), company registration, legal, insurance. Revenue model per service line (fixed-price projects, monthly retainers / managed services). 12-month projection, break-even, pricing tiers. |
| Conception | Architecture of the company's own platform at 3 scales (MVP on one PC → small cloud → scaled), as input to infrastructure costs. |
| Software & Cloud | Hosting cost options for each of those scales. |
| Marketing & Content | Market scan: AI-agent IT-services trends and competitors, with sources. |
| Sales | Target segments: which enterprises buy which service lines first, typical deal sizes, with sources. |

## Must research (do not assume)
1. Tunisian company form and registration costs for a virtual company, including domiciliation and the accountant.
2. Whether the company can get the Tunisian **Startup Act** label, and what it changes (taxes, social charges, foreign-currency account).
3. How a Tunisian company pays foreign SaaS subscriptions in USD and receives payments from foreign clients, including the practical limits.
4. The Claude plan path: Pro now → Max → API at scale, with the cost of each.

## Rules
- Every price is researched online and **cited with its source and the date it was read**.
- Legal and tax points are researched drafts for the founder to confirm with an accountant.

## Outputs
- `business-model.md`: the full business model, readable in 10 minutes.
- `costs.csv`, `revenue.csv`, `projection-12m.csv`: spreadsheets that open in Excel.
